# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

People being pitched an urban delivery concept: investors, partners, or
colleagues watching the scene live or in a recorded tour. They judge the idea
partly by how credible and polished the simulation feels.

## Product Purpose

A self-contained Three.js scene of one NYC block that demonstrates an
underground fulfillment-center-to-apartment delivery system: packages travel
from FCs through underground tubes, up a building conveyor, and are delivered
to individual rooms — alongside today's status quo (an Amazon van looping the
block). Success = a viewer immediately understands the concept and finds the
simulation believable.

## Brand Personality

Credible, smooth, quietly impressive. An engineering demo, not a video game.

## Anti-references

- Janky toy-like motion: objects snapping between headings, constant-speed
  starts/stops, limbs frozen mid-walk.
- Over-gamified UI (scores, confetti, arcade sounds).

## Design Principles

- Motion sells the concept: everything that moves should accelerate,
  decelerate, and turn like a physical object.
- The simulation is the hero; UI stays quiet and informative.
- Runs smoothly on a mid-range laptop in a browser tab with no build step.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Respect prefers-reduced-motion for camera flourishes; keyboard-reachable
controls; legible overlay text.
